# Context.md: Market Frenzy

## 1. Game Overview & Objectives
**Market Frenzy** is a digital supply chain and business strategy game designed as a "Kahoot-style" interactive experience. Players navigate a high-stakes market to buy ingredients, negotiate prices, and produce dishes to satisfy specific customer demographics. 

* **Game Length:** 5 rounds.
* **Round Duration:** 7 minutes of play + 3 minutes for the host per round.
* **Win Conditions:** There are two champions at the end of the game:
  * **Champion Seller:** The Seller group with the highest cash on hand.
  * **Champion Restaurant:** The Restaurant group with the highest cash on hand.

---

## 2. Parties & Roles
There are 4 distinct parties in the game:
* **Host / Supplier / Eater:** Controls the game flow, acts as the government/supplier selling wholesale ingredients, and plays the role of the Eater to buy the final dishes.
* **Sellers (3 Groups):** The middlemen who buy ingredients from the Supplier and sell them to Restaurants for a profit.
* **Restaurants (3 Groups):** The producers who buy ingredients from Sellers, cook dishes, and sell them to the Eater.

---

## 3. Game Set-Up Phase
**Host Responsibilities:**
* Set the number of groups and members per party.
* Set the initial cash-on-hand for all groups.
* Set the number of rounds and round duration (in minutes).

**Player Responsibilities:**
* Join the game lobby using a 6-digit code or QR code.
* Choose their respective party (Seller or Restaurant).
* Choose a name and avatar (optional) and join a group.

---

## 4. Game Flow

### Phase 1: The Opening Round
1. **Inventory Grant:** Each Seller group is given 3 free cards per type of ingredient.
2. **Eater Reveal:** The Host releases the randomized Eater Profile to all players.
3. **B2B Market:** Sellers set the selling prices of their ingredients and negotiate with the Restaurant party.
4. **Production:** Restaurants determine which dish to make, negotiate to buy ingredients from Sellers, and make exactly 1 dish to serve to the Eater.
5. **Selection:** The Host reveals which Restaurant's dish the Eater bought.

### Phase 2: Standard Market Loop (Repeats until game ends)
1. **Market Setup:** The Host/Supplier optionally adjusts round duration, releases a Random Event, and sets wholesale ingredient prices within the allowed ranges.
2. **Supplier Window:** Sellers purchase ingredients from the Supplier within a strict 2-minute window.
3. **Eater Reveal:** The Host releases the new Eater's profile.
4. **B2B Market:** Sellers set ingredient selling prices and negotiate with the Restaurant party.
5. **Production:** Restaurants determine which dish to make, buy the ingredients from Sellers, and make 1 dish to serve to the Eater.
6. **Selection:** The Host selects the winning dish and pays the Restaurant.

### Random Events (Market Shocks)
The regular price is usually the median price, but these events alter the economy:
* **(1)** All products are sold at the maximum price.
* **(2)** All products are sold at the minimum price.
* **(3)** All veggies are sold at maximum price, the others are sold at minimum price.
* **(4)** All meat products are sold at maximum price, the others are sold at minimum price.
* **(5)** All veggies are sold at minimum price.
* **(7)** All meat products are sold at minimum price, the others are sold at median price.
* **(8)** Buy two get one free!.
